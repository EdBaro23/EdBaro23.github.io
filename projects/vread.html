Personal project

VRead

A mixed reality reading application for Meta Quest. You turn the pages with your hands, and the reading tools raise reading speed by more than 50%.

Platform
Meta Quest, Quest 2 and Quest Pro
Built with
Unity 2022.3, Meta XR SDK 63
Role
Sole developer
Source
EdBaro23/VRead

What it does

A book sits in your real surroundings in mixed reality and you read it there, turning pages by hand rather than with a controller. Reading tools layered on top raise reading speed by more than 50% over reading the same text unaided.

Interaction

  • Hand-tracked page turns: the book watches hand position and triggers a turn when your hand crosses a threshold at the page edge, so the gesture is the one you would use on a physical book.
  • Animated pages: each page runs its own turn animation and cleans itself up once the turn completes.
  • Auto-reset: the book returns to its resting position after a period of inactivity, so it never ends up stranded somewhere you cannot reach.

Build

  • Unity 2022.3 with Meta XR SDK 63, built for Android on ARM64 with ASTC textures.
  • Requires hand tracking, anchors and scene support, which is what lets the book be placed against the room rather than floating in a void.
