What they are

Each demo takes one mechanic, system or platform feature and builds the smallest thing that proves it works. They are not finished games, and they were never meant to be. Building small and often is how I learned the engine.

What the range covers

  • PC: keyboard and mouse or gamepad, where there is room to be careless with performance and focus on the mechanic.
  • Mobile: touch input and a much tighter performance budget, which changes what a mechanic can afford to do.
  • VR and MR: hand and controller input, room-scale movement, and the comfort constraints that come with a headset.

Why it mattered

The XR projects elsewhere in this portfolio rest on this. Fifty-odd prototypes is where the Unity and C# work stopped being something I had to look up.
